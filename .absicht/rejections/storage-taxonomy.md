Enumerating storage technologies — key-value, relational, document, columnar,
object, queue, stream — is a schema change waiting to happen. C4 refuses the
taxonomy and keeps the technology as a string; ArchiMate's complete
node/device/system-software taxonomy is the warning, organised around an
axis absicht does not care about. A string never needs migrating.
